# CrawlScan reference visual pass

2026-10-09. Reference: https://crawlscan.fun/. The user explicitly asked to apply its visual
direction and make moving spiders the main detail. No portable `design.md` was requested.

## Observed reference

Read-only browser inspection at narrow and desktop widths showed a left-aligned large sans
headline, bright green emphasis, mono utility labels, almost-black surfaces, thin panel borders,
an inline contract input/action, compact recent scans and generous spacing between sections.
The site declares Geist for display/body and JetBrains Mono for labels. Computed paper was
`rgb(7, 9, 11)`, heading `rgb(238, 241, 243)`, and green control tint used `rgb(0, 200, 5)`.
These are reference observations, not production tokens; our colours remain semantic OKLCH tokens.

Spiders are an independent fixed canvas layer with `pointer-events: none`. Visible anatomy:
outlined green abdomen/head, eight long articulated legs, pale blue joints, glowing green feet,
and faint movement traces. They move around text and interface elements. A screenshot alone
does not establish the reference's simulation algorithm; ours is independently implemented.
The source contains its own token/reward claims and illustrative wallet data. Those are not
part of the CrawlSpider brief and are not copied into the product.

## Applied direction

Hallmark studied-DNA: Marquee Hero adapted to keep the scanner directly accessible;
edge-aligned brand/menu and mast-headed footer. Preserve installed Space Grotesk / IBM Plex Mono,
all routes, public scanning, real reports, evidence semantics and English interface.
Update existing tokens and append the visual layer to the global stylesheet.

The original `spiders.tsx` draws six crawlers on desktop, four on medium screens and two on small
screens. Feet plant and step separately from the body; jointed legs follow heading/form/report
bounds. Trails are decorative and do not represent wallets or analytical evidence. No dependency,
remote asset, copied JavaScript or source-site analytics is added.

The fixed control explicitly says “Ambient crawlers” outside scans. During a scan, job state
and phase drive the activity display and movement; terminal states stop the movement. Polling/SSE
remain the source of job state. There is no timer-based fake progress or invented verdict.
User pause persists in local storage. OS reduced-motion hides the moving layer. Rendering
suspends in background tabs, caps device pixel ratio at 1.5 and draws at most 30 frames per second.
The layer is decorative, inaccessible to pointer input and hidden from assistive technology.
Evidence dialogs use the native top layer, above decorative motion.
